Ingredients
1. The Sweet Potato Dough
2 lbs sweet potatoes (or Cuban boniato), peeled and cubed

1–2 whole star anise pods (for boiling infusion)

1 tsp pure vanilla extract

2 tbsp granulated sugar or brown sugar

3–4 tbsp cornstarch (acts as the primary binder)

The Spice Blend:

1/2 tsp cinnamon

1/4 tsp nutmeg

1/4 tsp ginger

A tiny pinch each of cloves, allspice, and cardamom

1/2 tsp salt

2. The Filling
4 oz guava paste (pasta de guayaba), cut into small 1/2-inch cubes

4 oz cream cheese, cut into small 1/2-inch cubes (keep chilled)

3. The Breading & Frying
1/2 cup fine white rice flour (serves as a micro-thin, non-clumping moisture barrier)

2 large eggs, beaten well

3/4 cup fine graham cracker crumbs (about 5–6 standard sheets crushed)

3/4 cup panko breadcrumbs (adds a shattering, rustic crunch and prevents the sugar from burning)

1.5 tbsp sugar and 1/4 tsp cinnamon (mixed into the crumb blend)

Neutral oil (canola or vegetable) for deep-frying

Step-by-Step Instructions
Step 1: Boil and Infuse
Place the cubed sweet potatoes and whole star anise pods into a large pot of lightly salted water.

Bring to a boil and cook for 15 to 20 minutes until completely fork-tender.

Drain thoroughly, discard the star anise pods, and let the potatoes sit in the warm pot for 1 minute to allow any excess steam to evaporate.

Step 2: Mash and Spice
Mash the sweet potatoes until completely smooth and free of lumps.

Mix in the vanilla extract, sugar, salt, and your full spice blend (cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, cloves, allspice, and cardamom).

Gradually fold in the cornstarch until a pliable, smooth dough forms.

Allow the dough to cool completely (pop it in the refrigerator for 20 minutes if it feels too warm or sticky to handle).

Step 3: Assemble and Fill
Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

Scoop out roughly 1/4 cup of the sweet potato dough, roll it into a sphere, and flatten it into a thick disc in the palm of your hand.

Place one cube of guava paste and one cube of cream cheese right in the center.

Gently cup your hand to bring the potato edges up and around the filling, sealing it completely with no gaps exposed, then roll back into a smooth ball.

Step 4: The Triple-Dredge (Rice Flour, Egg, Panko-Graham Crust)
Set up a breading station with three shallow bowls:

Bowl 1: Fine white rice flour

Bowl 2: Beaten eggs

Bowl 3: Mixed graham cracker crumbs and panko (with sugar and cinnamon)

Take an assembled ball and roll it lightly in the fine white rice flour. Because rice flour has a micro-fine consistency, it leaves a clean, powdery dust that seals the surface without clumping or pasty buildup.

Dip it swiftly into the beaten egg, letting any excess drip off.

Roll it firmly in the graham-panko mix, pressing gently with your palms so the jagged flakes and crumbs lock tightly into the egg-and-rice-flour base. Place onto the parchment-lined tray.

Step 5: Chill
Refrigerate the fully breaded balls for 20 to 30 minutes before frying. This crucial step firms up the starches and sets the exterior coating so it won't break apart in the hot oil.

Step 6: Fry to Perfection
Heat about 1.5 inches of neutral oil in a deep skillet or heavy-bottomed pot to 350°F to 375°F.

Fry the balls in small batches (do not overcrowd the pan) for 3 to 4 minutes, gently turning them, until the crust is deeply golden brown and ultra-crispy.

Transfer to a wire rack or paper towel-lined plate to drain.

Step 7: Serve
Let them cool for a few minutes so the molten guava-cream cheese center can settle slightly. Serve warm on its own or alongside a warm rum-caramel or vanilla cream glaze!
