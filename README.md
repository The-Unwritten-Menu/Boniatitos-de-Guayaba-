# Boniatitos-de-Guayaba
“A new Cuban-inspired dessert croquette, developed from the meeting point of papas rellenas, boniatillo, and guava-and-cheese pastelitos.”

---

* Prep time:  30 mins - 3 hours
* Cook time:  15 mins
* Chill time: 30 mins - 24 hours
* Yields:     12–15 balls

---

*This recipe takes the classic Cuban savory papas rellenas and reimagines it as a stunning dessert fusion. Featuring a fragrant, spiced sweet potato (boniato) base, a molten core of tropical guava paste and cream cheese, and a shattering-crisp crust made from a blend of graham crackers and panko, this dish bridges distinct pillars of Cuban comfort food into a modern sweet treat.*

---

#### Ingredients

The Sweet Dough
* cuban boniato
* sweet yam
* star anise
* vanilla extract
* turbinado sugar
* cornstarch
- fine white rice flour

The Spice is Right
- cinnamon
- nutmeg
- ginger
- cloves
- allspice
- cardamon

The Filling
- guava paste
- cream cheese

The Breading
- fine white rice flour
- eggs
- fine graham cracker crumbs
- panko
- sugar
- cinnamon
- grape nuts

The Frying
- canola or vegetable

***Step by Step***
1.  boil and infuse
1.  mash and spice
1.  assemble and fill
1.  triple dredge
1.  chill
1.  fry
1.  serve
